About Me

Hi again, I'm Conner. I'm a student at the University of Michigan, and I've been interested in computers for pretty much as long as I can remember. These days, I'm mostly focused on software development, but I'm also really interested in artificial intelligence and game development. I just enjoy building things, figuring out how they work, and seeing an idea turn into something real.

Outside of school, I try to spend as much time outside as I can. Sailing is probably my biggest passion and something I've been doing for most of my life. I also run and bike pretty regularly, which are both great ways to get outside and clear my head.

When I'm not doing any of that, I'm usually hanging out with friends and playing video games. It's probably my favorite way to relax after a busy day.
